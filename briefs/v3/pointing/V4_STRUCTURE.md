## 3. The shot template (every v4 shot)
1. ONE place, ONE action, 1.2-3.5 s. People small or medium: a head at most a QUARTER of the frame height (only the
   shot plan's `closeUp` shots go bigger).
2. The action = 2-3 HELD POSES that SNAP (CAST beats with `still: true`, cued on words). No reaching arm from off screen,
   no hand over a face, points sideways at a thing IN the frame, never at the lens. A free arm out in at most ~10 % of the
   film's frames.
3. At least THREE background movers that are not the main figure (walkers in depth, a turning / blinking prop, the
   world outside a window). No floating dust specks.
4. The camera: ONE glide (0.3-1.2 %/frame on screen), then a REST of 15-40 frames with no camera motion (film.ts freezes
   the end of every shot anyway: `camkit.withRest`).
5. Captions as before: keep the action out of y 1260-1440 (65.6-75 % of the height).
6. Opaque cards only (no character at an opacity between 0 and 1), one-frame card swaps on turns.
Example that compiles: `v4/sets/_template_example.ts` (one driver: look -> point snaps -> face lands; two walkers + a
blinking lamp; `pushRest` camera with a 24-frame rest).
