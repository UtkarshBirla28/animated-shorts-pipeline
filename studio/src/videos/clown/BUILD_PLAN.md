## What this film must do to the viewer
1. Frame 0 already looks like a thumbnail: a purple-and-yellow clown mid-ride on a unicycle, centred, in a sunny brick
   square. A real-world sound (bike horn / unicycle squeak) and the first word land inside the first 0.3 s.
2. By 7 s the viewer has a job: count the people on a phone. The narrator keeps telling the story while they count
   (listening + counting = the same split attention as a phone call).
3. The viewer spots the clown (the decoy) and feels clever.
4. The viewer does NOT notice three dollar bills on a branch that has been in the shot since frame 0, even though two
   counted walkers duck under it.
5. The story pays off (1 in 4), the reason takes two short sentences, the money study is told, and then the camera pulls
   back from the money branch into the opening framing: it was here all along. Last words: "Did you see the money?"
6. The loop is the proof: on the replay the bills are the first thing they look at.

## Pace and look rules (binding)
- Something new for the eye about every 2 s (a new framing, an entrance, a gag), camera always drifting gently; no still
  hold over 3 s; moves are slow bells (<= 45 px/f at 1080); dissolves 6-8 f or a continuous move; hard cuts only for
  punch (the turn-around, the tally). After a jump to a new angle wait ~1 s before a key fact.
- Every clause is shown literally on its word (the professor when "professor" is said, the clown suit on "clown suit",
  music headphones on "music", the bills on "three dollar bills").
- Gestures >= 12-18 f with anticipation and settle; expressions held >= 12 f; gaze goes to the action.
- Boards <= 4 words, caps >= 40 px, held >= 1.5 s, never inside the caption band (y 1280-1440) or the app's UI zones
  (keep key things inside x 60-930, y 180-1560).
- Colour: a sunny spring afternoon. Warm red-orange brick, fresh green leaves, clean blue sky, teal and cream accents.
  The clown's purple + yellow is the most saturated thing in every frame he is in. The bills are pale green with a
  white border against clean sky: plainly visible, never glowing before the reveal.
- Characters: the channel's flat cartoon cards with ink outlines, big readable faces; every person has a distinct
  silhouette and colour.
- The channel's crow is hidden once in the film (not near the bills), as in every video.

## The ONE set: the campus square (all shots are angles inside it)
A large herringbone red-brick plaza ringed by brick-and-glass university buildings (a library with tall windows, an arts
building, a clock on one facade), lamp posts with college banners (plain colours, no real logos), a bike rack, low brick
benches, a small fountain far back, evergreen hills and a blue sky with slow clouds behind.
- THE SCULPTURE (right of centre): our own design, a big tilted dark-blue cube with round cut-outs resting on three brick
  piers (generic; do not copy a real artwork exactly). The clown circles it.
- THE TREE (left edge, foreground): a deciduous tree with fresh spring leaves; ONE branch bent down and across the
  upper-left of the opening frame, over the diagonal path, at walkers' head height. THREE one-dollar bills are clipped
  along it with wooden clothes pegs (each bill ~120 px wide in the opening framing, a tiny white note taped to each),
  swaying slowly in the breeze.
- THE PATH: the main diagonal across the square, lower-left to upper-right, passing under the branch (walkers there are
  ~300 px tall in the opening framing, so they visibly dip their heads).
- THE STUDY CORNER (far end of the path): a small folding table with a plain sign "PSYCH STUDY", two interviewers with
  clipboards and lanyards.
- Life: pigeons pecking and hopping, a few far students crossing or sitting on benches, a frisbee arc far back, banners
  and leaves moving, cloud shadows drifting over the brick, a sprinkle of fallen blossom on the bricks.

## Cast
- THE CLOWN: vivid purple and yellow suit (big buttons, a ruffled collar), large red shoes, a bright red nose, a small hat,
  white gloves; on a UNICYCLE (one spoked wheel that really turns, pedals, a tall seat post). He pedals (legs alternate),
  wobbles for balance (arms out, +-5 deg), circles the sculpture at a steady pace, squeezes a bulb horn, waves.
- THE HERO ("phone girl"): teal hoodie, orange backpack, ponytail, a yellow phone at her right ear, chatting and
  laughing. She is phone-walker no. 3 in the count, the one who "walked by like he was invisible", who turns around,
  and one of the two who duck under the branch.
- FOUR MORE PHONE-WALKERS: each a different body colour and phone colour (red, white, blue, pink), phone held clearly to
  the ear, mouth moving. One of them is the second person who ducks.
- TWO MUSIC LISTENERS: big headphones (green, orange), head-bobbing.
- A PAIR OF FRIENDS: side by side, talking to each other, one gestures.
- ONE OR TWO WALKING ALONE.
- THE PROFESSOR: tweed jacket, round glasses, clipboard, kind and curious; stands by the sculpture during the opening.
- TWO INTERVIEWERS at the study corner (clipboards, lanyards).
Count rule: EXACTLY FIVE people with a phone at the ear cross the opening shot during the count. Nobody else holds
anything to an ear. Each phone-walker is fully in frame for >= 2 s and never hidden behind another person.

## Shots (frames come from words.ts / beats.ts: key everything to BEAT names, never literal frames)
Script lines are quoted from SCRIPT.md (v4, final).

### 1. THE SQUARE: hook + count (frame 0 to the end of "Easy.", about 0-21 s) ONE continuous set-up
- Framing: a slightly high 3/4 wide of the square. Upper-left: the tree and its branch with the three bills over the path.
  Right of centre, middle distance: the sculpture with the clown circling it, the clown ~240 px tall, the brightest thing
  in frame. The diagonal path runs from lower-left under the branch toward the far study corner. Faces and bills stay out
  of the caption band. The camera creeps (a slow push + a few degrees of arc); NO cut, dissolve or fast move in this shot.
- "A clown rode a unicycle around this square, and half the people never saw him.": frame 0 the clown is mid-ride,
  centre-right, horn honk at ~0.2 s; walkers already crossing.
- "Your turn. Count the people on a phone.": big clean text top-right "COUNT THE PHONES" (2 lines, holds 1.6 s, away
  from the branch), then a small chip "PHONES: ?" stays top-right until the answer. No phone-walker has left the frame
  before this line ends.
- The count (from "Count the people on a phone." to "How many phones?"): the five phone-walkers cross one by one, spread
  evenly, along the path and one cross-path; phone-walker 2 ducks under the branch about a third of the way through, the
  hero (no. 3) is mid-frame at the midpoint, phone-walker 4 ducks at about two thirds; the music listeners, the pair and
  the loner are mixed in between. The clown keeps circling.
- "A psychology professor needed something nobody could miss.": the professor (by the sculpture, clipboard) strokes his
  chin, watching the walkers.
- "A student of his owned a unicycle.": the clown pats the saddle / pops a tiny wheelie. "And a clown suit.": he spreads
  his arms in a ta-da, horn honk. "You don't waste a student like that.": the professor gives a proud thumbs-up.
- "So for an hour, he rode laps around the sculpture.": he pedals on, a full lap; the clock on the facade spins an hour.
- "How many phones? Five.": the chip flips to "PHONES: 5" with a ding. "And the clown? You saw him. Easy.": a short
  push toward the clown; he looks at the lens and waves.
- HIDDEN LAYER: the bills sway on the branch the whole time. No highlight, no sound, no glance toward them.

### 2. WHO DIDN'T? (lines "So who didn't?" / "The team stopped people leaving: did you see the clown?", ~4 s)
- The study corner: a medium of an interviewer (clipboard, friendly) stepping up to the pair of friends as they leave.
  In the background the clown is still circling, small.

### 3. THE TALLY (lines "People in pairs mostly said yes." / "So did people with music.", ~4 s)
- The friends both point back at the clown, laughing. Cut-in on the clipboard: a row "PAIRS" fills with ticks (7 of 10).
- A music listener (big headphones) bops up, nods, thumbs-up toward the clown; row "MUSIC" fills 6 of 10.

### 4. INVISIBLE (lines "But to one group, a purple and yellow clown was invisible." / "The ones you counted: people
on a phone call.", ~6 s)
- A tracking medium beside the hero on her call. The clown rides right alongside, waving, honking: in this shot his
  body is a see-through dotted outline (how she "sees" him), his honk muffled. Funny and clear.
- "The ones you counted": a quick pull that shows other phone-walkers too, each ignoring the outlined clown.

### 5. ONE IN FOUR (lines "[beat] Just one in four saw the clown." / "Many spun around to look.", ~4 s)
- The clipboard: row "ON A CALL": one tick, three crosses, each landing on the beat; a small sting.
- The interviewer points behind the hero. She lowers the phone, spins around (a 14 f turn with anticipation): the clown,
  solid and bright again, two metres away, waves. Her jaw drops (held).

### 6. THE REASON (lines "Your eyes stay on the path." / "Your mind takes the call.", ~3.5 s) ONE picture idea
- The hero walking, phone at her ear, eyes open and forward ("eyes stay on the path": two little dotted sight-lines
  to the path). On "Your mind takes the call": a see-through copy of her (her "mind") lifts out of her head and slides
  down the phone line into a small floating call bubble (a cosy kitchen where it chats with her mum); her body keeps
  walking, eyes blank. The square's colours drain a little as the bubble brightens.

### 7. THE MONEY (lines "So the professor tried again, with money." / "Three dollar bills on a branch, at head height,
over a path." / "Nearly everyone ducked." / "Few stopped for the cash." / "On a phone?" / "Six in a hundred.", ~10 s)
- "tried again, with money": the professor clips a bill to a branch with a clothes peg (hands close-up, a sly smile).
- A NEW-LOOKING ANGLE: a close, low view of a leafy branch over a path: sky, leaves and a hedge behind (the square, the
  sculpture and the clown are NOT visible). Three bills on pegs, close, each with its tiny taped note.
- "Nearly everyone ducked": walkers pass one after another and each dips under (a limbo rhythm, each a different dip).
- "Few stopped for the cash": one walker with no phone stops, delighted, reads the note on a bill.
- "On a phone? Six in a hundred.": the clipboard row "ON A CALL: 6 in 100"; phone-walkers duck and walk on, chatting.

### 8. THE DUCK (line "They ducked under the money and kept walking.", ~3 s)
- Slow motion: the hero ducks under, a bill brushes her ponytail, her eyes straight ahead, still chatting.

### 9. THE REVEAL + LOOP (lines "That branch has hung over this video since the first second." / "Two people ducked
under it while you counted." / "You saw the clown." / "[beat] Did you see the money?", ~8 s)
- From the branch close-up the camera pulls back and up in one move: the hedge slides away, the brick square opens, the
  sculpture and the circling clown come in: it lands exactly on the OPENING framing.
- During the pull-back the three bills pulse with a soft gold outline and a thin arrow; on "Two people ducked under it"
  see-through after-images of the two phone-walkers replay their ducks under the branch.
- "You saw the clown.": he waves. "[beat] Did you see the money?": the outline, arrow and after-images fade to nothing
  by the last frame.
- LOOP: the scene state is a function of the loop clock, so the state at the last frame + 1 equals frame 0 exactly
  (walkers, clown lap phase, clouds, leaves, bills' sway); the camera of shot 9 lands on shot 1's frame-0 camera with
  the same creep velocity. Verify: frame 0 and a render of "frame TOTAL" are identical, and the last frame flows into
  frame 0 with no jump.

## Sound (planned with the picture; SFX only on visible events, in word gaps, 0-3 f from the picture)
- Voice: James, ~3.4-3.6 words/s, gaps 0.12-0.20 s, two 0.3-0.4 s beats (before "Just one in four", before "Did you see
  the money?"). The two key lines a little slower and 2.5-3 dB up.
- First 0.3 s: a bulb-horn honk + the unicycle's squeak under the first word.
- Bed: the sunny square (birds, light breeze in leaves, footsteps on brick, far chatter, the fountain); the phone-walkers
  carry a soft muffled "blah-blah" as they pass.
- Music (original, no drums): a light, bouncy circus two-step (pizzicato bass, marimba / celesta, a muted-trumpet tune),
  ~18 dB under the voice. It thins to a tick-tock during the count, drops for the beat before "Just one in four", goes
  muffled (low-passed) when we are inside the hero's "call" (shots 4 and 6), turns curious for the money, and STOPS on the
  cut after "money?" (cut at the seam, no fade).
- Events: honks on "clown suit" and when he waves; pencil ticks and crosses on the tally; a ding on "Five"; a small sting
  on "one in four"; a whoosh as her mind leaves; leaf rustle + a soft whoosh for each duck; paper flutter for the bills;
  a rising shimmer on the reveal pull-back (the biggest sound in the film); NOTHING on the bills before the reveal.
- Master: -14 LUFS, true peak <= -2 dBTP.
