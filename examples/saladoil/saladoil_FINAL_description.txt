The Oil Tanks That Were Mostly Water
He Borrowed $150 Million Against Tanks of Water
The Salad Oil Scam That Helped Warren Buffett

DESCRIPTION:
In 1963, Tino De Angelis, New Jersey's "salad oil king", borrowed over $150 million against tanks of soybean oil. But most of those tanks were full of water, with a thin layer of oil floating on top. When the scam collapsed, one young investor bought in while everyone else ran. His name? Warren Buffett.
