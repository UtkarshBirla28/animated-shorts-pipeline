## The idea in one line
The video TESTS the viewer while it tells the story. Job given in the first seconds: "count the people on a phone".
Decoy: the clown on the unicycle (everyone expects him and spots him). Really hidden: three dollar bills on a branch
over the path, in frame from frame 0, with two phone-walkers ducking under it during the count. The ending tells the
viewer, and the loop is the proof.

## Facts (binding; sources in the research notes)
- Hyman, Boss, Wise, McKenzie & Caggiano, Applied Cognitive Psychology 24 (2010). Western Washington University, the
  central brick plaza ("Red Square"), a pleasant spring afternoon, ONE hour. The clown (a student) "wore a vivid purple
  and yellow outfit, large shoes, and a bright red nose" and unicycled around the large sculpture beside the main path.
- 151 people were stopped as they left: asked if they saw anything unusual, then asked directly about the clown.
  Saw the clown (direct question): phone call 25 % (6 of 24), alone 51 %, music 61 %, pairs 71 %. Overall about half.
  "Many turned around ... to see what they had missed." Nobody tripped; do NOT say they bumped into things.
- It is the phone CONVERSATION (not texting, not the device): music listeners noticed him.
- Hyman, Sarb & Wise-Swanson, Frontiers in Psychology 2014: THREE one-dollar bills clipped to a branch bent over a narrow
  path at head height. 396 walkers. "Noticed" = stopped to look or took a bill: phone users (talking or texting) 6 %
  (4 of 63), everyone else 20 %. Only 12 of 396 walked into the branch (so "nearly everyone ducked" is about ALL walkers).
- The student owning a unicycle and a clown suit comes from Hyman's press interviews (NYT Well blog, Oct 2009, as
  quoted by a student paper): keep it as narration, no quotation marks.
- Never say: "texting" for the clown study; "everyone else saw him"; a year for the ride; "three-dollar bill"; that the
  viewer "failed"; any statistic about the viewers of this video.

## The in-video test (binding)
- Opening set-up of the square is ONE continuous shot from frame 0 until "You saw him. Good." (no cut or dissolve while the
  count runs). Exactly FIVE people talking on a phone held to the ear cross during the count, each clear on a small screen;
  also two with headphones, one pair of friends, one or two alone.
- The clown rides in early, big, bright and central, circles the sculpture for the whole count, waves on "You saw him."
- The money branch: a tree at the left edge, one branch bent across the upper-left over the path; three $1 bills clipped to
  it (each about 120 px wide at 1080, pale green with a white border, on clean sky), swaying slowly, in frame from
  frame 0 and for the whole opening shot. Never covered by captions or the app's buttons. No sound, glance, camera move
  or highlight points at it. TWO phone-walkers visibly dip their heads under the branch during the count.
- The ending pulls back from the money close-up to the exact opening framing (same tree, same place): frame 0 = last frame.
- Honest: on a rewatch the bills are plainly there for the whole shot.
