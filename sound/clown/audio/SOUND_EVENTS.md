# SOUND EVENTS of "The Clown Nobody Saw": what the sound expects from the picture

Written by `sound/clown/audio/mix.py` at every run (inputs hash `9091d11a1bf0`), from the cue list in `sfx.py` and the score in `music.py`. Clock: `timeline.json`, 1868 frames at 30 fps = 62.267 s. Frames below are video frames (the picture clock is the voice's).

**How to use it.** Every sound is keyed to a BEAT expression (`suitEnd-1` = BEAT.suitEnd - 1; `S9.a+8` = SHOTS[8].a + 8), never a literal frame, so a voice re-time moves it with the picture. The last-but-two column says what should be happening in the picture on that frame (0-3 f tolerance). If the action lands somewhere else, either move the action, or put the frame in `sound/clown/audio/picture_events.json` as `{"<picture event>": <frame> | "beat+n" | null}` (null drops the sound) and re-run `python mix.py`. Kinds: **sync** = a hit on a visible action; **long** = a sound that runs with an action from its key to its end key; **life** = background life (a pigeon), free to move.

## Continuous sounds that follow the picture
- **The unicycle** (tyre on brick + a gentle pedal squeak on every wheel turn; in the bed stem). Its level follows the clown's real distance from the opening lens (`sfx.py UNI_FOLLOW_PICTURE = True`: near inside 15 m, far beyond 21 m); the hand envelope `UNICYCLE_ENV` is the fallback / override (0 = on the far lap, 1 = at the lens): `0` -> 1, `square-1` -> 1, `people+3` -> 0, `twoPeople+5` -> 0, `clown32End-5` -> 1, `END` -> 1; resolved frames: 0, 60, 170, 1700, 1790, 1868. Near level -25 LU, far lap -35 LU; pan +0.25 near the lens, else the clown's screen x on his route (`plan.ts clownAt`, via picture_plan.json). The squeak is timed from the metres ridden over the picture's wheel radius (`spin = s / WHEEL_R`, 0.3105 m): one 'eek' each time the wheel angle passes a whole turn + a softer 'ook' half a turn later (tell SOUND the crank phase of the down-stroke and it is one constant). Per shot (dB on the far level; None = not in the shot): 1: 0.0, 2: -3.0, 3: -3.0, 4: -3.0, 5: -2.0, 6: -12.0, 7: None, 8: None, 9: 0.0. It drops out with everything else for the beat before 'Did you see the money?' (he may hold his wave for those frames).
- **The phone-walkers of the count** (`phone_walker_1..5`): level and pan follow `cast_plan.ts WALKERS` (kind 'phone') frame by frame, read through `picture_plan.json` (exported from the picture's code at every mix: a re-timed walker needs nothing but a re-run). `{"phone_walker_3": null}` in picture_events.json drops one.
- **The bed** (birds, leaves, steps, far chatter, the fountain) is re-balanced per shot and opens up over the reveal's pull-back, which is taken to land on the opening framing at f 1766 (`pullback_lands`, default BEAT.youSawThe + 2).
