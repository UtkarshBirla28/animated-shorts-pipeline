## 4. The end and the loop seam

**Does the end earn a replay?** Partly.
- For it: the film ends 0.10 s after the last word, on the laugh, in the same shot as frame 0. A viewer has no
  picture cue that it ended, and "His bag is on that train. He is not." plays as the narrator's tag on "Bag...
  gone." Most people who reach the end should stay to about 3.0 s of the second pass.
- Against it: at 3.05 s of the second pass the hard cut to the car tells everyone it restarted, and before that cut
  there is nothing new to find. The two rewatch details are the boy's hand in the door window (the fact judge
  says it will not read at phone size) and the green dot (after the cut). So the loop is worth about 3 s per
