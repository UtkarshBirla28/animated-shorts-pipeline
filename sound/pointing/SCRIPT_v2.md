### 5.4 Picture and culture guards (from the panel, which I agree with, plus mine)
- No eating on the commuter train (no noodles and no boxed lunch, so not even the panel's "grandma with an ekiben":
  ekiben are for long-distance trains). No makeup gag.
- Any Japanese text must be real and correct (a made-up station name in checked kana and kanji), or there should be no
  text. No JR, MTA or team logos; a plain "NEW YORK" T-shirt.
- The tourist is likeable and dumb, and the joke is on him, not on Japan. (A Japanese viewer will comment "he'll get it
  back at lost-and-found". That's a good comment; let it happen.)
- Pacing (owner's note): every framing holds at least 2.5 s, medium or wide framings, no whips. The bag in the doorway
  and the 6-vs-1 jars must be readable at phone size.
