- **Picture:** the last shot IS frame 0's shot, 1.9 s earlier: the same camera, the same push-in and drift, the same

  simply the next frame. A continuous cut, not a match cut. The door window passes him once, in this shot only (in

- **The boy:** after his cap push he is pointing at the suitcase again, at the leading edge of the window, in the

- **Sound:** the music stops dead on "gone."; the train's motor whine runs straight through the seam with no fade;
