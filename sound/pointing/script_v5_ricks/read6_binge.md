## The three versions of one film (Q, S, V)
Post V.
- Opening: V and S share the strongest opening. They start on the game cold ("Tap your foot on every green light. Only green."). Q's opening is the worst of the three because it tells you about the red light before the test, so the test can't catch you.
- V over S: V's pivot "But the shouting didn't start as science." promises a twist and pays it off with "He never knew about the eyes." S's "And where did it come from?" promises nothing. V also squeezes the lab into "A hundred lights, then red." and runs 67 s against 71. S's past tense ("watched", "pressed") and "In 1994" are slightly cleaner, but they make it feel like homework.
- V over Q: everything after the opening is the same, so V wins on the opening alone.

The one change for V: rewrite the New York setup line so it works by ear. Something like "New York copied it, so conductors must point at a sign on every platform." That's the setup for the best laugh in the film, and right now it's the clunkiest line.
