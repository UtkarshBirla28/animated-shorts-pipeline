# Read 4: binge (19, US, 2 hrs of Shorts a day)

## My ranking, best first
1. **Tree crash (target fixation).** A guy builds a business with a lawn chair, then the sheriff hits the stump. Every line makes it weirder, and I only get the answer at the end.
2. **82-hole cart.** "Are we fighting them or grating cheese?" I have no clue what he's building until the rockets go. Perfect payoff.
3. **Pink cell.** "Barbie's gonna love it in here" plus the arm wrestle got me. The 15-minute twist is real "no way" material.
4. **Anti-theft sign.** The paperclip fiancee hook is great and so is "stolen twice". The middle gets a little like a lecture.
5. **Clown.** The quiz gets my thumb off the screen, and "did you see the money" gets me. The middle has too many people talking.
6. **Pointing v4.** Better than I expected, but it is still a fact video dressed as a story. Its best moment is a side story near the end.

**Position of the new script: 6 of 6.**

## Line by line
- **L1** "This train driver's eyes had gone bad. So he started shouting." This makes me tilt my head but it doesn't stop me. "Bad eyes" is too soft to hook me. Better: *"Every train driver in Japan points at nothing. It started with one guy's bad eyes."* That gives me the weird thing I'd see in the picture.
- **L3** "The story goes, his name was Hori." The hedge comes right at the start again. Cut it to *"His name was Hori."*
- **L5-6** The boss loving it without knowing the reason got a small smirk. That's good.
- **L7** "And they point, too." This is flat, and it gives away the only visual mystery the video has. Better: *"A century later they still shout. And now they point at it, like it owes them money."*
- **L9** "But why would good eyes need a trick for bad ones?" I had to work out "good eyes / bad ones" in my head. It's a riddle, not an itch I need scratched. Better: *"So why do drivers with perfect eyes still do it?"*
- **L10** "In 1994, Japan's railway lab set a trap." **This is where I swipe (around 22 s).** A year plus "lab" tells me a science lesson is coming. Better: *"So scientists tried to make people mess up on purpose."*
- **L12-15** I counted the numbers: 100, 12, 5, 10, 3, 12, 0. Too much math. "Five pressed anyway" is a small "no way". Cut L14.
- **L16** It does answer L9, and "your hand stops waiting for your brain" is a good line. It works.
- **L17** The cereal-in-the-fridge line is relatable. I nodded but didn't laugh.
- **L18** "Pointing and saying it makes it wait." Who is "it"? Better: *"Pointing makes your hand wait for your eyes."*
- **L19-22** The "dead sexy" sign is the one **real laugh**, and "had to point" lands. But it feels like it comes from another video, and it's the best thing in this one.
- **L23** "Or so the story goes." The ending shrugs, and it drops energy right after the laugh. Better: *"All because one driver's eyes went bad... and his boss never noticed."* That pays off the opening and the boss joke.

## What separates it from my top three
The top three are each one guy doing a weird thing that keeps getting weirder, and the reason only comes at the end. This one shows me the weird thing (pointing) at 20 s, then explains it with a button test. After that it adds a funny epilogue that has nothing to do with Hori. There's no build and nobody is in danger. Lots of people have already seen "Japanese drivers point" clips, so it's not new to me.

Edits can help: lead with the pointing, cut the lab to one number, end on the boss. That gets it to about 4th. A button test won't ever feel like an army at the gate, though. The gap is mostly **the material**, not the wording.

## Ready to record?
**Yes, after the small fixes above** (L1, L3, L9/L10, cut L14, L18, L23). It's recordable and won't embarrass anyone. Just don't expect it to beat the other five.
