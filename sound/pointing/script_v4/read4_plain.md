# Read 4, plain viewer (ESL, sound low, reading captions)

## My ranking (most want to watch first)
1. **Pink prison cell.** One room, one rule, and it gets stranger every line. The ending flips everything. I followed it from the captions alone.
2. **Tree crash.** The sheriff hitting the stump made me laugh out loud. One place, and the trouble keeps growing.
3. **Anti-theft sign.** "We'd better get ours now" is a great opening. The stolen-twice ending is clever. Easy English.
4. **Clown.** The counting game grabs me in the first second, even with no sound. "What clown?" is funny. The money question at the end got me.
5. **Pointing (new).** Clear, with a real "no way" in the lab part. But it is three small stories joined together, and the first one is soft.
6. **Mine salting.** The story is strong, but at low volume I lost who was talking (buddy, expert, rich guy). "Salting", "bucks", "rolls up" and "vein" were hard words for me.

## The new script, line by line
- **Line 1.** "Eyes had gone bad. So he started shouting." I am curious, and I would stop scrolling. It is not as strong as "never kept a paperclip", though.
- **Line 3.** "The story goes, his name was Hori." Here it tells me "maybe this isn't true" in the third second, and I relax. Better: *"Legend says his name was Hori."* Even better: put the doubt only at the end.
- **Lines 5-6.** "He never knew Hori's eyes were bad." A small smile. Nothing is at risk, though. Nobody is close to crashing.
- **Line 7.** Fine. "Over a century later" is clear.
- **Line 9.** "Why would good eyes need a trick for bad ones?" I had to read "bad ones" twice to understand it. I sort of want the answer. Clearer: *"Today's drivers can see fine. So why do they still do it?"*
- **Lines 10-15.** This is the best part. "Five pressed anyway" was my "no way" moment. "Three of them did it again" made me laugh. "Not one pressed" lands. It works with no sound because the boards say 5 OF 12 and 0 OF 12.
- **Line 16.** Yes, it answers line 9. "Your hand stops waiting for your brain" is the best line in the script.
- **Line 17.** Cereal in the fridge: I smiled, it's relatable.
- **Line 18.** "Pointing and saying it makes it wait." I don't know what the second "it" means. This line is dead. Better: *"Pointing and saying it out loud makes your hand wait."*
- **Line 19.** In India a "conductor" is the bus ticket man. The picture saves this.
- **Lines 20-22.** "Dead sexy... had to point" got a real laugh. But it is a new story right when the eyes story should be closing.
- **Line 23.** "Or so the story goes." It ends weak. It doubts itself again, and after the New York joke it feels tacked on. Better: *"All because one driver hid his bad eyes from his boss."* This brings back line 6 and gives the loop a little sneakiness.

The shouts ("Signal clear!", "Clear!") only work if the captions show who is shouting.

## Would I finish it? Share it?
I would finish it, because the lab hooks me at line 10. Lines 3-8 are the risky part, and I might swipe around line 5. I wouldn't watch it again. I might send it to a friend who travels by train. My comment would be: *"the cereal in the fridge is literally me"* or *"12 people and 5 still pressed lol"*.

## What separates it from my top three
Each of my top three has one situation in one place. Things get worse, and then there is a twist in that same world (the pink cell, the stump, the conscience pile). This script has three worlds: a 1910 steam cab, a 1994 lab and a 2013 New York platform. The opening story is a legend that the script itself doubts, and nothing goes wrong in it.

Edits help a little: cut the hedge in line 3, fix lines 9, 18 and 23, and make Hori hide his eyes so there is a stake. But most of the gap comes from the material. The real story is the lab trap, and the hook is a soft legend sitting in front of it. Starting on the trap ("12 people, one job, and 5 failed") might close more of the gap than any line edit.

## Ready to record?
Yes, after the small fixes to lines 3, 9, 18 and 23. It is clear and honest, and it is a solid middle video. It is not top three.
