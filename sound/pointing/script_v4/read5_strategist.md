# Read 5: strategist (blind A/B)

## Ranking of all seven (the one I'd most want to watch first)
1. **Cart (Ricks Clips)**: "82 holes" gives you a mystery in the first second, then a cheese-grater joke, a slapped hand and a human umbrella. You keep watching to see what it is, and the payoff hits.
2. **Tree crash (Ricks)**: the first line is impossible to swipe past. Every beat makes it worse (the arrow, the tow truck guy in a lawn chair, the sheriff hitting the stump). The ending is the best one here.
3. **Pink cell (Ricks)**: Barbie joke, arm-wrestle "wait, what?", then the reversal at 15 minutes. It loses a little in the explanation.
4. **Anti-theft**: the honest fiancée whispering "We'd better get ours now" is a real hook. "Stolen twice" is a perfect closer. It earned its 91 %.
5. **B**: a play-along hook, the cereal-in-the-fridge laugh, the "dead sexy" sign and a loop that brings you back. It's held back by a trap most viewers will pass, and by a dead patch around 38-48 s.
6. **Clown**: the quiz plus "did you see the money?" is a strong rewatch trigger. The middle is a list of stats.
7. **A**: "Legend says his name was Hori" at 4 s admits the hook might not be true, and I swipe. The best part (the lab) only starts at about 30 s, after most people have left.

## A vs B
**First 3 seconds.** B stops the scroll. A direct order ("Tap your foot...") plus a big lamp makes you do something before you've decided whether you care. A's "eyes had gone bad, so he started shouting" is odd but slow, and the hedge right after it kills it.

**Would I play B?** Yes. Foot-tapping is smart because it doesn't touch the screen, so it doesn't pause the Short. What goes wrong: you tell me "don't tap on red" and then give me only five greens. I'm watching for the red, I stop, and "Did you tap?" gets a smug "no". The trap doesn't catch anyone. Five beats is no habit at all; the lab used 100. The fix: make the narrator's voice say "Go" on the red too, and speed the beats up. The voice then traps the foot. Also, the woman on screen failing is the real laugh, so lean on her.

**Where I'd swipe.** In A, at about 4 s ("Legend says") and again around 15-20 s (boss, rule, a century later; a history lecture before any test). In B, at about 38-46 s. Once the payoff ("pointing makes your hand wait") has landed, the steam-train origin plays like a footnote. "The story goes" plus a 15-word sentence is exactly where the 40-50 s dip happens.

**Laughs and "no way" moments.** B has more. Both have the clipboard drop, the cereal and "dead sexy". B adds the woman's hand slamming down on red, and "He never knew about the eyes" lands harder as a surprise origin. A spends its "no way" (5 of 12 pressed) after the swipe zone.

**Ending.** B wins. "Now try the lights again. This time... point." is an instruction, and the loop into B1 makes people rewatch to test themselves. I'd comment "I DIDN'T tap... my foot twitched though" or "the cereal in the fridge is my whole personality". A's callback to the boss who never noticed is a nice line, but it doesn't ask me to do anything.

## Winner: B
It puts the best material first and makes the viewer part of the test. The ending gives a reason to rewatch and comment. A is a better-told history, but nobody stays long enough to hear it.

## Must-fix in B
1. B2 "Go. Go. Go. Go. Go..." with a silent red: the viewer passes easily. Replace with **"Go. Go. Go. Go. Go. Go. Go!"**, faster, with the last "Go!" landing on the red.
2. B3 "Did you tap?": a lone question that most people answer "no". Replace with **"Did you tap? She did."** (the woman freezes on her red X).
3. B13 "And the story goes, the shouting began with a steam-train driver whose eyes had gone bad.": too long and hedged, right in the dip. Replace with **"Legend says it started with a driver who couldn't see the signals."**
4. B9: two ideas in one breath. Split it: **"After a hundred times, your hand stops waiting for your brain."** / beat / **"That's how the cereal ends up in the fridge."**
5. B20 "This time... point.": a viewer pointing at the phone may touch it and pause the Short. Replace with **"Now try again. This time, point at it... and say 'red'."**
