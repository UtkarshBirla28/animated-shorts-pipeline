## Line by line
- **1** "This train driver's eyes had gone bad. So he started shouting." It works. A strange cause and a strange result makes me stop scrolling.
- **3** "At least, that's the story." This stops the energy right after the hook. Put the hedge into the name: **"Legend says his name was Hori. He shouted every signal to his coal man."**
- **7** "He never knew Hori's eyes were bad." It gets a small smile and it should be the start of a running joke, but the script never comes back to it. Sharper: **"He thought it was discipline. It was Hori's eyes."**
- **8** Fine. This is the point where I would swipe, because the Hori story stops building and we jump 100 years.
- **10** "But does it actually work?" Any video could say this line. Better: **"It looks ridiculous. So does it work?"**
- **11-16** Good. The 98, 99, 100... RED beat works. "Not one pressed" was my "no way" moment.
- **14** "Twelve people only looked at the light." "Only looked" confused me for a second. Better: **"Twelve people just watched. Five pressed anyway."**
- **17** "your hand stops waiting for your brain. Pointing makes it wait." This is the best line in the script. Keep it.
- **18** "Ever stared at your stove, left... and had no idea if it's off?" The tenses don't match and the line drags. Better: **"Ever left home and wondered if the stove was off? Point at it."**
- **20** "So the boss was onto something... by accident." It sounds like an ending, and then the video keeps going. Cut it.
- **21-24** The real laugh. "And the conductors... had to point." lands.
- **25** "Hori's boss would be so proud." This is a weak last line after the biggest laugh. The hook promised bad eyes, so pay that off: **"All because one man's eyes had gone bad."** Or keep the boss and make him the joke: **"Hori's boss? Still taking notes."**

## Does the ending pay off the start?
Not yet. The hook is about bad eyes and shouting. The ending is about a boss being proud of pointing, and Hori never pointed. Bringing back the bad eyes at the end ties it together and makes the loop worth it.
