1. **Cart and 82 holes.** Every line gets weirder; "human umbrella" got a laugh. The rockets pay off all of it. I'd send it.
2. **Tree on the empty road.** The tow truck guy in a lawn chair, then the sheriff hitting the stump. Funniest of the six.
3. **Pink cell.** "Barbie's gonna love it in here." The skinny guard wins the arm wrestle, and the ending flips the whole thing.

5. **Pointing v4 (new).** The lab trap and the "dead sexy" sign are good. The opening and the ending are soft, and it feels like two videos.
6. **Clown.** The quiz hook grabs me and "did you see the money?" makes me rewind. But the middle is five voices and a pile of stats.

## Line by line
- **L1-2** "eyes had gone bad. So he started shouting." Fine, and it makes me curious. But it's slow for a first line.
- **L3** "At least, that's the story." This is where my thumb moves. You hooked me, then told me it might not be true. Cut it, or move the hedge to the end of the Hori part.
- **L4-7** The coal man shouting it back with a grin is cute. "He never knew Hori's eyes were bad" is a small smirk. Good.
- **L8-9** The jump to today is clean.
- **L10** "But does it actually work?" is a filler question. Better: "So does yelling at a traffic light actually do anything?"
- **L11-16** This is where it got me. A trap, 100 lights with no red, then red, and five hands slam down. Then three of them do it again ("no way"), and not one person who pointed pressed..
- **L17** Clear. Keep.
- **L18** "Ever stared at your stove, left... and had no idea if it's off?" The wording is clunky and it kills the pace. Better: "Leaving the house and can't remember if the stove's off? Point at it." Or cut it.
- **L20** "So the boss was onto something... by accident." I got lost here. What was the accident? The idea is that he copied a fix for bad eyes without knowing it. Say that: "The boss copied a fix for bad eyes, and it turned out to work for everyone."
- **L21-24** The New York sign is the one real laugh I had. "And the conductors... had to point" lands.
- **L25** "Hori's boss would be so proud." Weak. The hook was bad eyes, and the ending never comes back to them. Better: "All because one guy needed glasses."

## Hook / ending
L1 is a reason to pause, not a reason to stay. Better: "This train driver's eyes were going. So he started yelling at the signals."
The ending does not pay off the beginning. It pays off the boss, and I never cared about the boss.

## Would I...
