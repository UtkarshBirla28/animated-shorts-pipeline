## The hedges
They are honest. "The story goes" opens the very next narration sentence, about 4 s in. "Or so the story goes" closes the film, so the tale has a hedge at both ends. A small risk: "The story goes, his name was Hori" can sound as if only the name is in doubt. "That's how the story goes. His name was Hori." covers line 1 more clearly (optional). Never put "his eyes went bad" in the title, the description or the thumbnail as plain fact.
