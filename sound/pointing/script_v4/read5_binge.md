## Must-fix in B
1. **B1 "Don't tap on red."** Gives the trap away. → "Tap your foot on every green light. Only green."
2. **B2 "Go. Go. Go. Go. Go..."** Too few, and the red is silent. → Eight quick "Go"s, getting faster, and the red lands *on a spoken "Go"*: "Go. Go. Go. Go. Go. Go. Go. GO." That pits ears against eyes, which is the real trap.
3. **B3 "Did you tap?"** → "Be honest. You tapped." (Once fix 2 is in, most people will have tapped.)
4. **B13 "And the story goes, the shouting began with a steam-train driver whose eyes had gone bad."** Slow and hedgy right where I'm about to leave. → "And supposedly, it all started with a train driver who couldn't see."
5. **B14 + B15** Narrate and shout less. Drop the narrator's "who checked it... and shouted back", because the picture plus "Signal clear!" / "Clear!" already says it.
